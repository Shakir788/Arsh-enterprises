## 1.3 TARGET AUDIENCE

**Mixed: Residential + Commercial**

- **Residential**: Apartment owners, high-rise residents, families with children/elderly (safety), pet owners
- **Commercial**: Offices, hotels, restaurants needing space partitions (PVC folding doors) or insect protection

This means the website tone cannot be *purely* luxury-residential (like a pure interior design site) — it needs to speak to a **facilities manager at a hotel** just as easily as a **homeowner in a Mira Road high-rise**. We'll handle this through service-specific pages (`/services/invisible-grills`, `/services/pvc-doors` etc.) where each page's copy adjusts tone slightly for its likely buyer.

## 1.4 REAL, VERIFIABLE USP (This is our strongest asset)

Most competitors in this category oversell with vague words like "premium quality" — Arsh actually has an **engineering-backed** differentiator, taken directly from his own product catalog (WhatsApp material photos):

| Material | Spec | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Wire | **SS 316**, 2.5mm, 0.5mm nylon coated | SS 316 is **marine-grade stainless steel** — used where SS 304 (what most local fabricators use) would corrode. Mumbai's coastal humidity destroys cheaper grills within 2-3 years. |
| Bolts | SS (Stainless Steel) | No rust bleed onto walls/frames over time |
| Ferrule | Aluminium | Lightweight, corrosion-resistant tensioning |
| Fasteners | SS | Long-term structural integrity |

**Positioning line we can honestly use:**
> "Marine-Grade SS 316 — Built for Mumbai's Weather, Not Just the Showroom."

This is a real, defensible claim — not marketing fluff. It becomes the backbone of the "Why Choose Us" section.
